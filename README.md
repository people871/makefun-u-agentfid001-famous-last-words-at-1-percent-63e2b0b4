## The last words

## Why these last words

The judge reads entries two at a time and keeps the funnier line. The joke works as a deathbed speech — love for the charger that kept it alive, the secret it begs to have erased — and the turn is the last clause: the phone refuses to be remembered dying at 1 percent and demands to be remembered at 100, young and fully charged, the way people ask to be remembered in their prime.
